# Car Insurance Policy: SafeDrive Basic

## Coverage
- Accident damage to the insured car
- Theft of the insured car
- Fire and natural disaster damage

## Limits
- Maximum payout per claim: Rs 5,00,000
- Deductible (customer pays first): Rs 5,000

## Rules
- Claim must be reported within 30 days of the incident.
- A police report is required for theft, and for any claim above Rs 50,000.
- Photos of the damage are required for accident claims.

## Exclusions (NOT covered)
- Driver was drunk or under drugs
- Driver had no valid licence
- Racing or stunt driving
- Damage done on purpose
- Normal wear and tear (old tyres, rust)
- Driving outside India
